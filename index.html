Who this is built for

The strongest fit is familiar technology, unfinished go-to-market.

A cybersecurity or identity security company with real technology and real market opportunity — and a commercial system that hasn't caught up yet. Any of this will sound familiar:

Selling has moved beyond the founder, but the next-stage commercial model isn't defined yet.
Revenue performance is uneven, and leadership isn't sure which part of the system is the constraint.
Strong technology isn't translating consistently into buyer urgency or commercial outcomes.
Sales, marketing, product, solutions, partnerships, and customer success run on different assumptions.
Senior GTM capability is needed now — without hiring a full permanent executive bench.
The business is heading into a fundraise, new market, launch, or hiring surge.

Method

Five phases, one accountable engagement.

01

Diagnose

Examine the GTM system end to end and separate root causes from visible symptoms.

02

Identify the few constraints that matter most and align leadership on sequence and ownership.

03

Define the strategy, operating model, process, or capability required to close the gap.

04

Build & operate
