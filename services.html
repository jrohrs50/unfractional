Engagement model

Framed around your need, not a fixed construct.

Unfractional presents one accountable offering. The internal mix of people may vary by engagement — core team members, associates, or contracted specialists — but the client experiences a coherent team, a shared method, and one integrated outcome.

ModelBest when
AdvisoryOngoing executive guidance, decision support, and coaching — capable internal leaders need experienced perspective and pattern recognition.
Diagnostic / strategy projectA defined assessment, strategy, roadmap, or transformation plan — the problem needs to be clarified before execution begins.
Capability buildDesign and implementation of a specific capability — positioning, sales process, value selling, partner motion, technical evaluation, enablement, or customer lifecycle.
Fractional leadershipEmbedded, part-time ownership of a function or mandate — active senior leadership is needed, but not a full-time permanent hire.
Interim leadershipTemporary operating responsibility during a transition, vacancy, restructuring, launch, or critical growth phase.
Integrated transformationA coordinated engagement spanning multiple GTM functions, supported by the right combination of team members and specialists.

Method

How Unfractional works.

A consistent engagement method gives clients clarity while allowing scope and delivery model to stay flexible.

01

Diagnose

Gather evidence from leadership, teams, customers, partners, data, processes, and current operating rhythms. Distinguish root causes from visible symptoms.

02

Prioritize

Identify the few constraints that matter most now. Make tradeoffs explicit and align leadership on sequence, ownership, and measures of progress.

03

Design

Define the strategy, operating model, process, capability, narrative, tool, or organizational change required to close the priority gaps.

04

Build & operate

Work alongside the client to implement, test, reinforce, and adjust — providing embedded or interim leadership where appropriate rather than stopping at recommendations.

05

Transfer & scale

Establish sustainable internal ownership, documentation, cadence, and skills. Help the client hire or transition responsibilities as permanent capacity becomes appropriate.

Possible first step

The GTM diagnostic.

A structured, low-risk entry point for a company that knows growth isn't working as expected — but hasn't yet identified the underlying constraint.

  • Leadership interviews and a review of strategy, goals, pipeline, customer journey, operating cadence, and current roles
  • Assessment across market, revenue, technical, partner, delivery, and customer-success dimensions
  • Identification of root causes, dependencies, and near-term risks
  • Prioritized recommendations and a sequenced 90- to 180-day roadmap
  • Options for project-based, fractional, interim, or internally led execution
