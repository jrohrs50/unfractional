About

Built from scaling one GTM system, start to acquisition.

Unfractional helps technology and services companies strengthen and scale their go-to-market systems — across the full commercial lifecycle, from market strategy and pipeline creation through sales execution, technical validation, partnerships, customer success, and organizational scale.

What Unfractional is

Strategy and hands-on execution, in one accountable engagement.

The offering combines strategic perspective with hands-on execution. Engagements may be advisory, project-based, fractional, interim, or delivered through an integrated team. The form is flexible; the objective is consistent — help leadership teams make better GTM decisions, close critical capability gaps, and create a more coherent, repeatable path to growth.

We look across the entire customer lifecycle to identify the gaps limiting growth, then provide the strategy and hands-on leadership needed to address them.

What "fractional" means here

Fractional describes one way clients can access the offering — not the offering itself. Unfractional may provide embedded, part-time leadership when that's what a company needs, but it may also deliver a focused assessment, a capability build, an interim mandate, or ongoing executive advisory. The company isn't limited to filling named part-time roles.

Origin

The GTM through-line.

Unfractional was shaped by leaders who worked together while at multiple early-stage product companies to help evolve into a more mature global businesses, culminating in multiple funding rounds and acquisitions.

That experience showed that scaling go-to-market takes more than improving one team at a time: market positioning, sales execution, technical credibility, partnerships, product alignment, customer outcomes, and organizational design all have to evolve together.

This shared proof point and origin of the model is not the only source of credibility. The broader evidence comes from the team's collective experience across cybersecurity startups, growth-stage software companies, global vendors, consulting organizations, partner ecosystems, enterprise customers, and later-stage transformation roles.

The differentiator

Most outside support enters through a single functional lens: sales, marketing, product, technical strategy, partnerships, or customer success. Unfractional is designed to determine how those pieces interact — and where the misalignment among them is what's limiting growth. The value isn't simply access to several experienced people; it's an integrated way of diagnosing and strengthening the GTM system, from executive strategy through practical implementation.

  • Creating repeatable sales, technical, partner, enablement, and customer-facing motions

  • Developing global partnerships, alliances, and routes to market
  • Leading across startup, growth-stage, vendor, integrator, and enterprise environments
